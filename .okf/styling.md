# Styling Conventions

- **Borders**: All primary UI panels and the navbar use a 1px border with a dark grey color (`border-[#333333]`).
- **Shadows**: Drop shadows are disabled across all panels and the navbar to maintain a clean, flat design aesthetic.
- **Corners**: Generally rounded (`rounded-3xl` for main panels, `rounded-b-[32px]` for navbar).
